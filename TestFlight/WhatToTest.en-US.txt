Uniboard 1.3 beta.

LAYOUTS
Settings > Layouts adds base keyboards (Latin, Greek, Cyrillic), with your device's languages first. Drag to reorder, swipe to remove; one always stays. With two or more, the space bar names the current one: hold it, slide and lift to switch. A tap still types a space. Check Shift and Caps Lock across a switch, Turkish i/İ, Greek ς and Ά, 12-key Ukrainian and Hungarian rows, and that the keyboard remembers the layout with Full Access OFF.

GESTURES
Hold gives the layout's accents first (French é è ê ë, Russian ё, Greek ά). Up gives superscript then subscript; Down gives styled forms: mathematical Latin, mathematical Greek (𝛂), or related Cyrillic letters (є э for е). Groups never repeat across Hold, Up and Down. Customize Keys has a layout menu.

PAGES
The Pages tab sets up pages: keys, variants and the page Star opens. Add Text adds each typed character as its own key. Arrange Keys places keys in rows with half-width and full-width gaps; check the keyboard draws the same rows. The Pages menu exports custom pages to a file and imports one as new pages. In the keyboard, the list button in the page strip drops down a menu of pages.

TEXT AND EMOJI FORMS
Characters with both presentations (☃, ⌚) show as text in charts and side by side in details, with a copy button each. The picker adds each form separately. In keyboard search they appear twice, once per form; each inserts its exact sequence, and holding a page key offers the other form.

KEYBOARD UNICODE
Result and browse keys use rounded keycaps with the character's name in small text, no U+ code. Browsing without keys makes the keyboard two rows taller; search and typing keep their height.

EXPLORE
Tiles are 2 to 7 per row: pinch to change. Tapping a block's name in the chart opens that block, with a block navigator at its top.

EDITIONS AND SIZE
Only the Unicode 19 draft is bundled. Unicode 18, 17 and 16 show the draft limited to characters and emoji of that version: check that a newer character reads as unassigned, What's New lists only that version, and newer blocks are absent. The app is much smaller.

FONTS
Preview basics (Unifont, Noto Sans Symbols 2) now download on demand in Settings > Font Downloads, like the other packs. With Extra Unicode fonts on and the pack downloaded, check U+0558 previews in app and keyboard with Full Access OFF. Test cancel, retry, remove and offline use.

PRIVACY AND FAVORITES
App Favorites reach the keyboard with Full Access OFF; keyboard saves wait locally until optional Full Access permits writing back. Typing, queries, pages and favorites are never sent over a network.
