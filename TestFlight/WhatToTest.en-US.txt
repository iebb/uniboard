Uniboard 1.3 beta. New in this build:

FONTS FOR THIS CHARACTER
In a character's details, View Fonts compares the fonts Uniboard has: packs, your fonts and downloaded Google Fonts. Each preview is drawn only with the face it names, never with a fallback, and fonts that can't draw the character are listed apart. Open a face for its details and styles. Check a few scripts and symbols, and that Italic, Bold and other style icons match the face.

GOOGLE FONTS
Browse Google Fonts, in Settings > Font Downloads or from a character's fonts, finds families by name. Download a single style, then check that it appears in the comparison, survives relaunch and offline use, and can be removed. Removing a download keeps an identical font you added yourself. The keyboard never goes online for fonts.

COPY IMAGE
With Text Preview on, the preview's actions offer Copy Image. It copies only the text shown in the preview, as a picture at 3x; typing and the host text are unchanged. Choose Black on White, White on Black, Black on Transparent or White on Transparent; transparent choices really are transparent (the checkerboard isn't copied). Check that the choice is remembered, that the button stays visible at the bottom of the panel, and paste into Notes, Messages or an image editor.

ARRANGING KEYS
In Pages and Pages > Keyboard, a held key lifts as the same key and the others make room as you drag. Nothing is saved until you drop; keys are never lost or duplicated. Check with Reduce Motion on as well.

EXPLORE TILES
Pinch now goes past 7 per row while tiles stay at least 32pt wide (9 on most iPhones, more on iPad). Small tiles and block charts show the Unicode version as a small corner triangle instead of the ribbon. Check pinching back in and that the setting is remembered.

STILL WORTH CHECKING
Layouts and the space bar switch, Hold/Up/Down gestures, text and emoji forms, keyboard search, ConScript, Unicode editions, and Favorites with Full Access OFF behave as in the previous beta. Nothing you type is sent over a network.
