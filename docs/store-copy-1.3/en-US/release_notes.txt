Explore fonts, inspect Unicode text and make your keyboard your own.

• Compare available fonts, filter by family or style, and download Google Fonts on demand.
• Set preferred fonts for code points, ranges and Unicode blocks.
• Inspect normalization forms and UTF-8/UTF-16 values, with copy actions.
• Copy preview text as an image with custom colors, transparency and 1×, 2× or 3× resolution.
• Edit and rearrange number and letter keys, choose automatic capitalization, and transfer your setup with TOML import and export.

Font choices affect Uniboard’s previews, not other apps. Copied images paste as pictures, not editable text.
