Find and type Unicode symbols, special letters and emoji with a tap. Uniboard is an offline character browser and custom keyboard for iPhone and iPad.

- Search by official character name, alias, descriptive note or block name. Try "arrow", "heart" or "Greek".
- Look up code points in hex (U+1F680, 0x1F680 or 1F680) or decimal (decimal:128640). Paste a character into the app's search to identify it.
- Browse the complete Unicode block list and scroll through character grids, from arrows and math symbols to alphabets and phonetic marks.
- Type directly from the keyboard. Search, tap to insert, or hold a character to inspect and save it.
- Keep favorite and recently used characters close at hand. The app and keyboard keep separate collections on your device.
- Inspect a character's name, block, available annotations, decimal value, category and UTF-8 bytes in the app.
- Copy individual characters or combine them in the scratchpad, then copy your text.

Works offline, without an account or Full Access. Uniboard does not send your typing to a server.

To enable the keyboard, open Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard, then choose Uniboard. While typing, hold the globe key to switch to it.

The catalog is built from the latest official Unicode draft available at build time. Draft names and assignments may change. Character display depends on your iOS version and fonts; some newer characters may appear as boxes. Preview markers for invisible characters and combining marks are not inserted.

The interface and Unicode character names are in English. iOS uses its own keyboard in password and phone-number fields, and some apps disable custom keyboards.

Requires iOS 17 or later.
