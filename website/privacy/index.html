Privacy policy.

Uniboard is an offline Unicode character explorer and keyboard for iPhone and iPad, developed by Kitta Ltd.

Uniboard does not collect your typing or searches. It has no accounts, advertisements, tracking or analytics SDK. Its custom keyboard does not require Full Access.

Typing and character search

The Unicode catalog is bundled with the app. Searches run on your device. The keyboard keeps its search query inside the keyboard extension and inserts only the character you select into the active field.

Uniboard does not send your typing, search queries or inserted characters to Kitta Ltd or an app-operated server. After you insert a character into another app, that app controls the resulting text; its own privacy policy applies.

Data stored on your device

Favorites, recent characters and any scratchpad text you save are stored locally in the app's storage. The app and the keyboard maintain separate collections. These collections are not synced by Uniboard to a developer-operated service. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data. Device backups may include app data under your Apple backup settings.

Clipboard and keyboard permissions

When you choose Copy, Uniboard writes the selected character to the system clipboard. Other apps may access clipboard content under iOS rules. Uniboard does not continuously read or monitor the clipboard.

The keyboard works without Full Access. iOS controls which text fields allow a third-party keyboard. Uniboard does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or advertising permissions.

Support messages

If you contact Kitta Ltd for support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and your message. We use it to answer your request and resolve the issue. Do not include sensitive typing, passwords or private documents in a support message.

This website and external links

This static website uses no app-operated analytics, tracking scripts, third-party fonts or advertising cookies. Cloudflare Pages hosts the website and may process ordinary request information, including an IP address, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. Links to Apple, Unicode or other websites are governed by those services' policies.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from children or other users. It does not provide messaging, user accounts or a social network.

Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date. For privacy questions, use the contact details on the support page.
