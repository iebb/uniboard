Uniboard 1.3 beta.

LAYOUTS
Settings > Layouts adds base keyboards (Latin, Greek, Cyrillic), with your device's languages first. Drag to reorder, swipe to remove; one always stays. With two or more, the space bar names the current one: hold it, slide and lift to switch. A tap still types a space. Check Shift and Caps Lock across a switch, Turkish i/İ, Greek ς and Ά, 12-key Ukrainian and Hungarian rows, and that the keyboard remembers the layout with Full Access OFF.

GESTURES
Hold gives the layout's accents first (French é è ê ë, Russian ё, Greek ά). Up gives superscript then subscript; Down gives styled forms: mathematical Latin, mathematical Greek (𝛂), or related Cyrillic letters (є э for е). Groups never repeat across Hold, Up and Down. Customize Keys has a layout menu.

PAGES
The Pages tab sets up pages: keys, variants and the page Star opens. Add Text adds each typed character as its own key. Arrange Keys places keys in rows with half-width and full-width gaps; check the keyboard draws the same rows. The Pages menu exports custom pages to a file and imports one as new pages. In the keyboard, the list button in the page strip drops down a menu of pages.

TEXT AND EMOJI FORMS
Characters with both presentations (☃, ⌚) show as text in charts and side by side in details, with a copy button each. The picker adds each form separately. In keyboard search they appear twice, once per form; each inserts its exact sequence, and holding a page key offers the other form.

KEYBOARD UNICODE
U opens search with its keys directly. Results stay in a row above the keys, also in landscape. With Text Preview on, the preview row stays above search. Result and browse keys use rounded keycaps with the character's name in small text, no U+ code. Browsing without keys makes the keyboard two rows taller; search and typing keep their height.

EXPLORE
Tiles are 2 to 7 per row: pinch to change. Tapping a block's name in the chart opens that block, with a block navigator at its top. Block pages also show 8 or 16 per row as a chart; on iPad, Explore and block pages add 32 per row, and charts use more of the width.

EDITIONS AND SIZE
Only the Unicode 19 draft is bundled; Unicode 18, 17 and 16 limit it to their version. Check that newer characters read as unassigned and What's New lists only that version.

FONTS
Preview basics (Unifont, Noto Sans Symbols 2) download on demand in Settings > Font Downloads. Check U+0558 previews in app and keyboard with Full Access OFF, and cancel, retry, remove and offline use.

EMOJI 18
The Emoji previews pack is now one Noto Color Emoji font with all of Emoji 18, including the leftwards and rightwards thumb signs (U+1FAF9, U+1FAFA) in every skin tone. An earlier download shows as not downloaded: download it again, then check Emoji 18 in the Emoji tab and keyboard.

YOUR FONTS AND TEXT PREVIEW
Font Downloads > Add Font File adds a TTF or OTF file; Use Installed Font picks a font installed by a font app. Either previews characters first, including Private Use, in the app and keyboard. Settings > Text Preview adds a keyboard row showing the text around the caret as Uniboard draws it.

CONSCRIPT (UCSUR)
Settings > ConScript (UCSUR) tints registry Private Use characters blue (try U+F8D0 Klingon or U+E000 Tengwar) in the app and keyboard; details keep the Unicode name and add a ConScript group. Prefer ConScript over PUA names them as the registry does, lists ConScript blocks in Blocks, finds "tengwar letter tinco" in search and draws them with Fairfax HD first. The ConScript pack appears in Font Downloads only while ConScript is on. With ConScript off, nothing of it shows.

PRIVACY AND FAVORITES
App Favorites reach the keyboard with Full Access OFF; keyboard saves wait locally until optional Full Access permits writing back. Typing, queries, pages and favorites are never sent over a network.
