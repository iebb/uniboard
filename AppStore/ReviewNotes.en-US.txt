Uniboard 1.3 is an offline international and Unicode keyboard for iPhone and iPad. No login is needed. Core search and typing work offline. Full Access is optional.

SETUP
Add Uniboard in iOS Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard. In Uniboard > Settings > Try Your Keyboard, focus the editor and hold the globe to select Uniboard.

DAILY INPUT
Literal typing has a number row (removable in App Settings), three letter rows and taller matching keys. Tap Shift for one capital, double-tap for Caps Lock, or touch Shift and slide to a letter. Test accents, punctuation, double Space, Return and repeating Backspace. There is no autocorrect or prediction. U toggles the Unicode toolbar. App Settings controls one optional symbol bar or its search-bar replacement. iPad has stock-style modifiers and compact/full ANSI landscape layouts for English; Numbers/Symbols and Hide Keyboard sit right of Space.

LAYOUTS (NEW)
App Settings > Layouts adds and orders base keyboards: English plus German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Swedish, Finnish, Norwegian, Danish, Polish, Czech, Hungarian, Romanian, Turkish, Greek, Russian, Ukrainian, Bulgarian and Serbian (Cyrillic). The space bar then names the current layout; hold it, slide to another and lift to switch. A tap still types a space. The keyboard remembers the layout without Full Access. Customize Keys has a layout menu for that layout's letters.

UNICODE SEARCH
Tap U and open Unicode/search. Search U+1F680 and tap the rocket result to insert its original scalar. Names, descriptions, block names, aliases and complete emoji are supported; search keys stay English. Both Delete controls edit the query while search is focused, never the host document. Done returns to host typing while keeping the query/results. Hold/hover a result to inspect without inserting. Save in details adds to or removes from Favorites; it never types into the host. Emoji insert as intact sequences.

PAGES AND GESTURES
The app's Pages tab creates, renames, orders and deletes pages, edits each page's keys, hold variants, and rows with half-width and full-width gaps, and picks the page Star opens (Favorites by default). In the keyboard, Star beside Space opens that page and returns to typing; the list button in the page strip opens a menu of pages. Swipe across all pages and Recent.

Each character key has Up, Down and Hold choices; a tap always types the key. Defaults: Up gives superscript then subscript forms; Down gives mathematical, circled, parenthesized, fullwidth and small-capital forms (mathematical Greek on Greek, related letters on Cyrillic); Hold gives the layout's accents. Once choices show, the press keeps that group; slide sideways to select. App Settings can change every list. Unicode editions and optional fonts are app-only settings: 18 release default, 17/16 previous, 19 DRAFT. The draft catalog is bundled offline; final editions are views of it.

PRIVACY
No typing, queries, accounts or analytics are collected. Shared on-device files contain app settings and layouts, configured keys, pages and explicit Favorites, never document typing or queries. The app writes these and the keyboard reads them with Full Access OFF. Optional Full Access permits writing Favorites saved in the keyboard back to the app and light iPhone haptics; it does not enable network collection. Recents and scratchpad stay local. iOS uses its own keyboard in secure/phone fields; apps may disable custom keyboards.

OPTIONAL FONT DOWNLOADS
App Settings > Font Downloads offers Noto-first historic/symbol, rare Han, Seal and emoji packs, singly or via Download All. Downloads are explicit, cookie-free static file requests to GitHub's release CDN; no typing, query or favorite data is sent. Files are size/SHA-256 verified before local App Group installation, excluded from backups, removable and usable offline without Full Access. Extra Unicode fonts defaults off; the Preview basics pack downloads on demand too.
