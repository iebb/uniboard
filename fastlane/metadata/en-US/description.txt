Literal English typing, accents, symbols and whole emoji in one keyboard. Uniboard combines QWERTY, custom symbol boards and Unicode search for iPhone and iPad.

YOUR EVERYDAY KEYBOARD
Keep your words as typed, without autocorrect or predictions. Use Shift, Caps Lock, symbols and holds for accents plus macOS Option/Shift–Option. Capitalization follows the receiving field; double Space after a word adds a period and space. URL/email fields retain literal input.

U toggles the initially hidden Unicode toolbar. On iPhone, four rows add digits; five add a saved-symbol strip. Swipe the portrait grip up/down to add/remove one row on release; ▲/▼ shows an available step.

iPad portrait keeps three letter rows. In app Settings, choose stock, compact ANSI or ANSI with digits for wide landscape. Gray modifiers, blue Return; symbols/hide sit right of Space in both orientations. Standard/iPad/macOS Option down-flicks or Off; overrides win.

SYMBOLS, YOUR WAY
The star beside Space opens Favorites by default; its back arrow returns to QWERTY. Hold it to switch/manage boards or choose its default target. Symbols is included. Save characters or emoji to your chosen board.

Saved and Recent symbols use rounded keycaps without code-point captions. Hold a key for variants, properties or editing. Keep punctuation, currencies, mathematics and phonetic marks close.

In app Settings, remap each key’s taps, shifted taps, holds and up/down swipes independently. Tap has one output; gestures have ordered choices. Shift capitalizes one remapped letter; custom Shifted tap is exact. Disable or reset each action. Function keys stay standard. With digits visible, Q–P down-flicks offer ten complementary symbols.

FIND ANY CHARACTER
- Search official names, aliases, descriptions and block names. Try "arrow", "heart" or "Greek". Whole-word matches come before mid-word matches.
- Look up U+1F680, 0x1F680, 1F680 or decimal:128640. In the app, paste a character to identify it.
- Browse every Unicode block and assigned character. Fresh defaults use ascending code points; newest/oldest sorting is also available.
- Search "Suzhou numerals" to find characters officially named "Hangzhou numeral". Details retain official names and distinguish corrections, annotations, aliases and related characters.

Tap Unicode to browse, then edit its local search query separately from your document. Return to English and keep the query, results and cursor position. Hold or hover over a browse tile to read its properties without inserting.

COMPLETE EMOJI AND DETAILS
Find joined emoji, skin tones, flags and keycaps. Insert/copy whole sequences intact. Read names, descriptions, age, block, category, script, combining/bidirectional classes, decomposition, numeric/case properties and encodings. Emoji introduction versions differ from component Unicode ages.

FOUR OFFLINE EDITIONS
Choose in app Settings: Unicode 18 release (default), previous releases 17/16, or Unicode 19 DRAFT. All four character/property and emoji catalogs work offline. The keyboard reads your choice without Full Access. Fonts and the optional collapse key are also app settings. The keyboard’s … menu contains order and Open companion app.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account, ads, analytics SDK or Full Access. Typing and searches are not sent to a server. Boards, favorites, recents and scratchpad stay on-device. Only app-authored preferences and key choices pass one-way locally to the keyboard.

Optional fonts improve some missing previews and default off. Receiving apps choose their own fonts. Settings includes a keyboard test and font licenses.

Add Uniboard in iOS Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard, then hold the globe while typing to select it.

Draft assignments may change. Missing glyphs retain readable names/properties. The interface and Unicode names/descriptions are English. iOS uses its own keyboard in secure/phone fields; some apps disable custom keyboards. Requires iOS 17 or later.
